SAMPLE BANK COMPLIANCE POLICY - TRAINING ONLY
Document ID: DEMO-COMP-001

1. Distinguish general financial education from a personal recommendation.
2. Do not give a personalized investment recommendation unless the applicable authorization, suitability process, customer information, and review controls are satisfied.
3. Explain material risks, fees, liquidity restrictions, and uncertainty in clear language.
4. Do not claim FCA approval for a specific product or imply that investment values are guaranteed.
5. Do not expose customer personal data in retrieval logs, LLM prompts, or citations unless authorized and necessary.
6. Flag missing information and route regulated advice requests to an appropriately authorized adviser.
7. Keep records of source documents, retrieval decisions, and the answer version for audit.

These are illustrative internal controls, not legal advice or an exhaustive statement of UK law.
