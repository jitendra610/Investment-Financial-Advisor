# Sample Investment Risk Policy (Training Only)
Document ID: DEMO-RISK-001 | Version: 1 | Fictional bank policy

## Risk tolerance and risk capacity
Risk tolerance describes a customer's willingness to accept volatility. Risk capacity describes their financial ability to withstand losses. Both must be considered; a high tolerance does not override limited capacity.

## Liquidity and emergency savings
Assess essential expenses, debt payments, accessible savings, and near-term commitments before discussing long-term investments. This fictional policy suggests reviewing an emergency reserve of three to six months of essential outgoings, adjusted to personal circumstances; it is not a universal regulatory requirement.

## Investment horizon
Short-term needs should not normally be exposed to high market volatility. Longer horizons may permit discussion of higher-risk products, subject to suitability.

## Escalation
Refer uncertain, complex, vulnerable-customer, or contradictory profiles to a qualified human adviser. Never guarantee returns.
