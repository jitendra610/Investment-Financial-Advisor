# Illustrative UK Investment Products (Training Only)
Document ID: DEMO-PROD-001

## Cash savings
Cash accounts generally avoid direct equity market volatility, but interest rates can change and inflation may erode purchasing power. Check provider terms and any applicable deposit protection separately.

## Diversified equity funds
Equity funds hold shares in companies. They can experience substantial market declines and do not guarantee returns. Diversification reduces concentration risk but does not eliminate market risk.

## Bond funds
Bond funds can fall in value due to interest-rate movements, credit risk, and market liquidity. They are not equivalent to cash deposits.

## Stocks and Shares ISA
An ISA is a UK tax wrapper rather than an investment asset. Tax treatment depends on individual circumstances and rules can change. Check current official limits before quoting them.

## Pensions
Pensions may offer tax advantages but have access restrictions. Specific tax and withdrawal rules must be checked against current authoritative guidance.

## Product assessment
Compare investment horizon, risk capacity, liquidity needs, costs, diversification, and tax considerations before discussing suitability.
